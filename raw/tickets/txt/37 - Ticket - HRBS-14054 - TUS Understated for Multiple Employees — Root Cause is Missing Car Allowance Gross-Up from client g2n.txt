TUS is coming in lower than expected for a set of employees receiving a car allowance. TUS is
7.5% of Taxable Total, so it's only ever as accurate as the gross feeding into it -- for these
employees, the car allowance itself is the problem, not TUS.

Several employees are guaranteed a fixed net car allowance (500,000, 625,000, or 750,000
depending on grade), and HRB correctly grosses that figure up so the employee still nets the
guaranteed amount after tax. CLIENT tracks most of these correctly, just under a separate
"Gross up" column rather than folding it into the Car Allowance line itself -- that's fine, just a
different structure, not an error. But for two employees, Yannick Kagi-banzouzi and Cyrille
Massamba, that Gross up column is empty entirely, and the car allowance sits at the flat 500,000
with no markup applied anywhere. That's the real gap, and it flows straight through into an
understated Taxable Total and TUS for those two.

Separate from this, two other employees have an unrelated productivity rate issue already
flagged previously, and three more show a "SS Employee Arrears" deduction that only appears
for them and nowhere else in the population -- that one looks like a CLIENT-side data entry
situation specific to those three individuals rather than a system issue, so we're not raising it
here.
