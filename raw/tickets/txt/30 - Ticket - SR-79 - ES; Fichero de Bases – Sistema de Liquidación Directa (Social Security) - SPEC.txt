                  Point 4 (B/P indicator): it doesn't act on the Amount field alone -- it's a per-Codigo gate on
                  the whole record. Settlement Data Type, Code, and Amount form ONE record for a given
                  Codigo. B means all three go out together; P means all three can be omitted together (or
                  included if applicable). You can't have Type+Code without Amount, or vice versa -- they're
                  gated as a unit, not independently.

                  @Lily Li @Drew Õispuu --

                  1. Codes 500/509 -- corrected using the real WTC wage types.

                  509's old formula used a generic flag (58235) that fires on ANY leave type, including
                  ordinary pago-delegado sick leave -- that's why it was overlapping with 500. Replaced it
                  with the specific wage types, each confirmed by its own WTC explanation text:

                  79006/79007 (maternity/paternity), 79029/79030 (risk during lactation/pregnancy), 79032
                  (parental leave) -- always pago directo, no delegado variant exists for these.
                  79417-79424 (abortion, disabling menstruation, termination of pregnancy) -- WTC
                  explanation literally states "INSS pago directo" for each one (Art.26bis/26ter ET, RD-ley
                  1/2023).
                  Left out of 509, staying under 500: 79415/79416 (ordinary sick leave, day 16+) -- WTC
                  tags these explicitly as pago delegado.

                  500's formula is unchanged except for one addition: it now excludes the 509 list above, so
                  the same base isn't reported twice.

                  New code added: C-563 (Pago Delegado IT, common-contingency origin) was missing
                  entirely, despite being mandatory alongside C-500 in every pago delegado case. Mapped
                  to 79415/79416.

                  Still open, not guessed: C-663 (work-accident equivalent of 563) and wage type 79012
                  (Accident Leave) -- no payout-level wage type found yet confirming delegado vs. directo
                  for accident-origin sick leave. Flagged in the spec, not resolved.

                  2. ReferenciaExterna -- confirmed, use Drew's version. Field is already documented as 1-
                     8 characters. The old 'BAS' + MMyyyy suggestion is 9 characters, which already
                     breaks that limit -- it was never valid. Drew's approach (last 4 digits of the CCC +
                     MMyy) is the one to keep.

                  3. CCC showing as zeros -- confirmed bug in the XML. Opened
