        Wallisson dos Santos Gomes Commented 3 days ago
        @Lily Li this is the expected behaviour, not a defect. Cese of the relationship (any reason,
        incl. baja voluntaria) is itself a regularización trigger under Art. 87.2.3º RIRPF; at the
        finiquito, the annual figure the zero-floor compares against gets recalculated to the real
        year-to-date total, not the original full-year projection. When that recalculated base minus
        the mínimo personal is zero or negative, the rate is zero (Art. 86.1/87.3) -- the earlier in the
        year the cese falls, the more likely that is. No retroactive fix to earlier payslips needed (Art.
        87.3 blocks restitution), and it's not blocked by the Art. 86.2 2% floor (that only applies to
        contracts originally agreed as duración inferior al año, not to an indefinite contract ending
        early).
