types, with references to specific legal acts, so the HRBlizz configuration team can build correct
logic into the system.

 1. Average earnings calculation

Payment Legal basis Note

1.1 Vacation pay Order 100 (12-month calculation period)

new trip; if paid as a regular workday, they are included

2. Employee gifts
Tax-exempt formula: non-taxable portion = 25% of the minimum wage (as of January 1 of the
reporting year), per subparagraph 165.1.39 of the Tax Code
The portion above this threshold is taxed as an additional benefit -- with gross-up (see section
3)
3. Additional benefit -- gross-up and coefficients

Both calculation variants are needed (the company needs to decide which to apply as the
standard):

Variant 1 (tax authority position): the natural coefficient applies only to the PIT base; military levy
() is calculated on the actual value, without a coefficient.
K(PIT) = 100 / (100 - PIT rate)
Variant 2 (extended): a single coefficient accounts for both PIT and military levy.
K = 100 / (100 - (PIT rate + military levy rate))

Coefficients must be built as a formula (not a hardcoded number) so they recalculate
automatically if rates change (including the separate Diia City PIT rate of 5%).
