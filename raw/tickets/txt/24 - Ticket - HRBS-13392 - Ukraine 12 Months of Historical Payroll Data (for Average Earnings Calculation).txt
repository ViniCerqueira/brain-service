Issue Description:

For correct payroll processing from day one of go-live, the system must contain 12 months of
historical payroll data (accrued earnings by month, by employee) prior to the go-live period.

Why this is required:

Under Ukrainian legislation, average earnings are not calculated from a single period -- they are
calculated based on the employee's actual earnings over a defined lookback period (calendar
months preceding the event), depending on the calculation type:

Sick pay / maternity (Procedure No. 1266) -- average earnings calculated based on the 12
calendar months preceding the month of the insured event.
Business trips, other guaranteed payments (Procedure No. 100) -- based on the last 2 calendar
months preceding the event.
Vacation pay -- based on the 12 calendar months preceding the month vacation begins.
Indexation (Procedure No. 1078) -- depends on the base month and requires historical
accumulated index values, not earnings history per se, but is similarly dependent on historical
records being present in the system.

If historical earnings data is missing or incomplete in the system, any average-earnings-based
calculation performed immediately after go-live will be incorrect, because the system will only
"see" earnings from the go-live month forward, not the required lookback window. This directly
affects sick pay, vacation pay, and business trip payments for any employee whose qualifying
event occurs in the first 12 months after go-live.

Hi @Nataliia Hahan ,

Confirmed the legal basis and defined the field-level requirements. This isn't one dataset
-- it's three separate elements, each tied to a different calculation:

12 months of monthly earnings (ESV-subject) -- needed for sick pay/maternity (
1266) and vacation pay ( 100, 12-month branch)
12 months of calendar days worked/excused -- separate denominator, specific to sick pay
( 1266) -- not the same as regular calendar days
2 months of working days worked -- separate denominator, specific to business trips and
other retained-pay events ( 100, 2-month branch)

Indexation needs no historical earnings -- it only needs the base month, already covered
by the existing "Effective Date of Current Salary" field.

All 3 added to the Data Dictionary (v3.7) under a new "Historical Payroll Data (Pre-Go-Live
Migration)" group, kept separate from regular EE-card fields since this is a one-time load,
not ongoing master data.

Thanks -- this breakdown looks correct overall, and the separation into three distinct
elements makes sense. Two clarifications before we finalize v3.8, as these affect how the
fields should be defined/labeled:

                   1. "Working days" for the 2-month branch ( 100 -- business trips/retained pay)

For this calculation, the denominator is the number of working days according to the
enterprise's work schedule for the 2-month period -- not the employee's actual
attended/worked days. Could you confirm whether the field currently captures the
scheduled working days rather than "days worked" in the literal sense? If the field is
labeled/interpreted as "days actually worked," it could produce an incorrect denominator
whenever an employee had any absence in those 2 months.

                   2. Exclusions applied to the 12-month earnings dataset

vacation pay ( 100, 12-month branch). However, the exclusion lists under these
