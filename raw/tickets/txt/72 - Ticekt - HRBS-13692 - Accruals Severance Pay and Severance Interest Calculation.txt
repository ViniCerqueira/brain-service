Severance Pay and Severance Pay Interest Consolidation

 1. Importance of Severance Pay and Interest Consolidation

In Colombia, companies are required to recognize and control the labor obligations accrued in
favor of their employees, including severance pay and interest on severance pay.

The consolidation process allows the company to determine the amounts accrued for each
employee as of a specific cutoff date. It also facilitates reconciliation between payroll and
accounting records and forms part of the information required for the payroll closing process and
client delivery.

 2. Severance Pay Calculation

Under Colombian labor regulations, severance pay corresponds to one month's salary for each
year of service, or the proportional amount for a fraction of a year.

For calculation purposes, the salary basis must consider the employee's salary and the payments
that legally qualify as salary under Colombian regulations.

For this company, the severance pay consolidation must consider:

Employee salary.
The average of the salary-related concepts accrued during the applicable period.
Employee hire date.
Days effectively accrued.
Severance pay payments or settlements made during the period, when applicable.

When variable salary-related concepts apply, they must be included in the applicable average
according to the rules governing severance pay calculations. Article 253 of the Colombian Labor
Code establishes that when the salary has varied during the previous three months or when the
employee has a variable salary, the average amount earned during the last year of service, or
during the time worked if less than one year, must be used.

3. Severance Pay Interest Calculation

Employees are entitled to interest on severance pay at a rate of 12% per year, or proportionally
according to the applicable period.

For consolidation purposes, interest must be calculated based on the accrued severance pay and
proportionally according to the corresponding period.

The general calculation is:

Severance Pay Interest = Accrued Severance Pay × 12% × Accrued Days / 360

Colombian regulations establish that this interest is recognized on the severance pay balance
and must generally be paid in January of the following year, or proportionally in the event of
termination or partial settlement of severance pay.

4. Severance Pay Provision

Currently, the system does not automatically generate the severance pay provision.

The provision is currently calculated externally by the payroll team and subsequently uploaded
into the system through an Excel file.

The following concept must be used for the severance pay provision:

22110 - Provision Severance - Social Benefits Provision Accrual 1

The system should automatically calculate this provision, considering the applicable salary basis,
the average of salary-related concepts, and the days accrued by each employee.

5. Severance Pay Interest Provision

Currently, the system also does not automatically generate the provision for interest on
severance pay.

The calculation is performed externally and subsequently uploaded into the system through
Excel.

The following concept must be used for the severance pay interest provision:

22112 - Provision Interest Severance Pay - Social Benefits

The system should automatically calculate this provision based on the accrued severance pay,
the applicable annual interest rate of 12%, and the corresponding accrued period.

7. Functional Requirement

The system should automate the severance pay and severance pay interest consolidation
process by allowing it to:

Calculate severance pay based on the employee's hire date and accrued days.
Determine the appropriate salary basis in accordance with Colombian labor regulations.
Consider the salary and the average of salary-related concepts, as applicable.
Calculate severance pay interest at an annual rate of 12%, proportionally according to the
accrued period.
Automatically generate the severance pay provision.
Associate the severance pay provision with concept 22110 - Provision Severance - Social
Benefits Provision Accrual 1.
Automatically generate the severance pay interest provision.
Associate the severance pay interest provision with concept 22112 - Provision Interest Severance
Pay - Social Benefits.
Consider payments and amounts previously recognized.
Automatically generate the consolidation report required for the payroll closing process and
client delivery.
8. Current Process and Supporting Documentation

Currently, the severance pay and severance pay interest provisions are calculated externally by
the payroll team and subsequently uploaded into the system through an Excel file.

Additionally, the system does not generate the consolidation report required for the payroll
closing process, so the report must be prepared externally for validation and delivery to the
client.

The severance pay and severance pay interest calculations are attached, along with the Excel
consolidation report currently generated and delivered to the client. These files provide a
reference for the current calculation methodology, calculation bases, provisions, and required
report structure.
