                  Wallisson dos Santos Gomes Commented about 1 month ago (edited)
                  @Lily Li @Michael Mukosi John -- both queries resolved. Details below; spec updated.

                   1. Report language (D1, D5, D6, 4DF) -- Lily

                  The reports must be developed in UKRAINIAN, not English. This is a statutory requirement.

                   2. Payroll  Sum Graf 16/17/18/19/20 (all rows) -- Michael

                  These fields ARE in the spec -- they're the table footer totals in Section D (TABLE
                  FOOTER /  ), D1 Spec rows 37-41, file positions 031-035, XML footer
                  elements R01G16 through R01G20. They were hard to locate because the BA Mapping
                  column described them by name ("Total of [field]") rather than as an explicit SUM formula.
                  I've now rewritten the BA Mapping to be explicit:

                  | Footer field | XML element | Spec row | BA Mapping |
                  | Total Accrued Gross Salary (all rows) | R01G16 | 37 | SUM(Graf 16 / pay element 58133)
                  over all EMPLOYEE_ROW records |
                  | Total Accrued Within ESV Base Limit (all rows) | R01G17 | 38 | SUM(Graf 17 / pay element
                  58130) over all EMPLOYEE_ROW records |
                  | Total Min-Wage Difference (all rows) | R01G18 | 39 | SUM(Graf 18) over all
                  EMPLOYEE_ROW records; 0 if no part-time top-ups |
                  | Total Employee ESV Withheld (legacy) | R01G19 | 40 | SUM(Graf 19) = 0.00 (employee
                  ESV abolished 01.01.2016, Law 77-VIII) |
                  | Total ESV Accrued by Employer (all rows) | R01G20 | 41 | SUM(Graf 20 / pay element
                  2550) over all EMPLOYEE_ROW records; must reconcile with ESV paid to DPS |

                  Mechanics: each footer total is the arithmetic sum of the corresponding per-employee
                  detail column (Graf 16-20, rows 25-29) across every insured-person row in D1 Table 1. The
                  DPS uses R01G16-G20 as control totals to verify the individual rows sum correctly, and
                  R01G20 is the key figure reconciled against the employer's actual ESV payment. R01G19 is
                  always 0.00 for any filing from 2016 onward (retained in the XSD for pre-2016 corrections
                  only).

                  Basis: MFU  (D1 table structure); Law 2464-VI; Law 77-VIII (2016 employee ESV
                  abolition); DPS XSD R01G16-R01G20.
