                  Nataliia Hahan Commented about 8 hours ago
                  @Lily Li @Mohit Jain @Wallisson dos Santos Gomes

                  In Ukraine, we have two payroll payments per month: advance and balance (final
                  payment).

                  "Advance" isn't quite the right name for it -- it's not really an advance in the sense of pre-
                  paying something. It's the payment for the first half of the month, for the days actually
                  worked in that period. In that sense, advance and balance are conceptually the same thing
                  -- both are wage payments for actually worked time. The difference is just when they're
                  calculated: the balance is the end-of-month calculation, done after we can prepare the full
                  monthly reports. For the advance, we don't need to prepare any reports.

                  Every earning type can, in principle, be used in either the first or the second half of the
                  month. Some employers simplify this by paying a flat amount each month as the advance
                  -- for example, 53% of the gross salary -- and do the full detailed calculation of all
                  earnings in the second part.

                  Sick leave and vacation pay can't wait until the end of the month to be paid -- by law they
                  must be calculated with all taxes applied and paid together with either the first or the
                  second part of the month, depending on when they fall.

                  In HRBlizz, this is reflected as two payroll runs:

                  1st payroll run -- calculates and pays the advance.
                  2nd payroll run -- includes all amounts already used/paid in the first part, plus an
                  additional column showing the net advance amount that was already paid mid-month (so
                  it isn't paid twice).

                  We also need to show the opening balance and closing balance for each month -- i.e.,
                  what hasn't yet been paid to employees. Controlling this balance every month is very
                  important.

                  Please let me know how I can help you here.
