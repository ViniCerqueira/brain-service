                  Wallisson dos Santos Gomes Commented 6 days ago
                  @Sivaranjani Subbarayula @Govind Thakur

                  1. No STR flag should be configured. ITS remains due.

                  The impôt des microentreprises (and the régime de l'entreprenant) is "libératoire des
                  autres impôts et taxes, à l'exclusion de l'impôt sur les traitements, salaires, pensions et
                  rentes viagères (ITS) ... aussi bien à la charge des salariés que de l'employeur" — DGI, Le
                  Système Fiscal Ivoirien. It replaces the entity's own patente, BIC/BNC and TVA. It has
                  never covered the ITS withheld on wages.

                  It is also inapplicable on the facts: the regime caps at 200 000 000 XOF of annual
                  turnover, and the January payregister alone shows 18 employees and 46 100 070 XOF of
                  earnings in one month.

                  If the client maintains the position, please ask for the specific instrument — a convention
                  d'établissement, an agrément, or a DGI ruling. A regime name is not an exemption.

                  2. Ceiling: 70 000 is correct.

                  Prestations familiales (5 %), maternité (0,75 %) and AT/MP (2–5 %) cap at 70 000
                  XOF/month. 75 000 is the SMIG — the assiette floor, not the ceiling. Pension caps at 3 375
                  000 (45 × SMIG). Source: CLEISS CI contribution sheet, updated 1 January 2025.

                  The client file is itself inconsistent: 17 employees computed on 75 000, one (Boris-Alban
                  Seka) on 70 000. Also note the columns labelled "CNAMGS" and "FNH" are Gabonese
                  bodies that do not exist in Côte d'Ivoire — the amounts are AT/MP at 3 % and PF+AM at
                  5,75 %.

                  3. Defects found in the GTN while reviewing (REF-P201-861, January) — these need
                  separate tickets.
