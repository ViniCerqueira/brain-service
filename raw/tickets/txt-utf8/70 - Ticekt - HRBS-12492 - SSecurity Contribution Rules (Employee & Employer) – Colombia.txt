Hi team,

We need the system to correctly calculate Social Security contributions (Health, Pension, ARL,
and Payroll Taxes) for Colombia, based on the legal rules explained below.

Entity ID:REF-LE-00-505

 1. Contribution Base (IBC)

Contributions are calculated on the IBC (Ingreso Base de Cotización) — the employee's salary
plus any payments that legally count as salary. The minimum IBC is 1 SMLMV and the maximum is
25 SMLMV.

 2. The 40% Rule (Law 1393 of 2010)

Non-salary payments (extralegal bonuses, allowances, etc.) cannot exceed 40% of the
employee's total compensation. If they exceed 40%, the excess amount must be added back into
the IBC, and contributions must be calculated on that higher value.

 3. Contribution Rates

Health is 12.5% in total, split between employee (4%) and employer (8.5%). The employer's 8.5%
is exempted when the employee earns up to 10 SMLMV.

Pension is 16% in total, split between employee (4%) and employer (12%).

ARL is paid only by the employer, and the percentage depends on the risk class assigned to the
job.

The Solidarity Pension Fund only applies when the IBC is 4 or more SMLMV, and it is paid only by
the employee. The percentage increases gradually depending on how many SMLMV the IBC
represents.

4. Payroll Taxes (Parafiscales) — Employer only

These are calculated on salary only (not on non-salary payments), and include Family
Compensation Fund (Caja) 4%, ICBF 3%, and SENA 2%. The employer is exempt from ICBF,
SENA, and Health when the employee earns up to 10 SMLMV.

5. Social Security During Vacations — Special IBC Rule

When an employee is on vacation, the employer must continue contributing normally to Health,
Pension, and ARL. However, the IBC used must be the salary earned in the month immediately
BEFORE the vacation started — not the vacation payment itself. This applies to both enjoyed
vacations and advance-paid vacations.

Legal basis: Art. 3, Decree 1072 of 2015, and PILA guidelines.
