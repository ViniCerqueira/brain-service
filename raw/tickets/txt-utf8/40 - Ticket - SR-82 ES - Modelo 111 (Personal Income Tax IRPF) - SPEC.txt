                  One usage rule

                  The quarterly window is a lookback from the payroll you generate from, not "the calendar

                      generated from February → Dec + Jan + Feb ❌ (crosses the quarter and the year).

                  Hi @Lily Li

                  Root cause: Company Legal Name (12-51, 40 chars) was actually two AEAT fields merged
                  into one — Denominación o Apellidos (23-82, 60 chars) + Nombre (83-102, 20 chars) = 80
                  chars. Missing 40 characters shifted every position after it, including 6 recipient-count
                  fields (9→8 chars) and IBAN (24→34 chars).

                  Full field table rebuilt against AEAT's official technical design (dr111e16v18.xls) —
                  confirmed against both the A3 sample and HRB's own output, byte-for-byte.

                  Amendment Type: for M111, two fields, Declaración Complementaria (538, "X"/blank) and
                  Número de Justificante de la Declaración Anterior (539-551, 13 digits) — populated only
                  for Supplementary/Substitute. Checked M190 and M216 too — both already correctly
                  document their own version (M190: Complementary Flag 121 + Substitutive Flag 122 +
                  Prior Declaration ID 123-135; M216: Complementary Declaration 432 + Prior Justificante
                  Number 433-445), no fix needed there.

                  Spec v4 updated.
