                  Wallisson dos Santos Gomes  Commented 22 days ago
                  @Lily Li

                  File updated.

                  Points 2 & 3 — BIK for Clave G: zeros is correct in practice, but map it as derived-zero, not
                  hardcoded. In-kind IS legally valid for clave G — the AEAT defines it as "percepciones,
                  dinerarias o en especie", and positions 108-147 carry no clave restriction. It's zero because
                  we have no BIK pay elements for Clave G perceptores, not because clave G excludes in-
                  kind. If it's hardcoded and a client ever pays a professional in kind, we'd under-report.

                  Related: the two incapacity in-kind fields (positions 282-321) are Clave A only — "solo
                  para percepciones correspondientes a la clave A". Not B, not G.

                  Clave B fields: added column X, highlighted in purple, marking every field with its AEAT
                  basis.

                  Applies to B (subclaves 01/03/04/99): identification fields, Income Type Clave/Subclave
                  (subclave mandatory for B), the four amount fields, the full personal/family block (Family
                  Situation, Spouse NIF, Disability, Children, Ascendants, Reduced Mobility — all "solo claves
                  A, B, C"), plus Irregular Income Reduction and Deductible Expenses.

                  B.01 only: Incapacity Gross Amount and Incapacity Withholdings.

                  Does NOT apply to B (marked red): Contract Type, Contract End Date, Geographic
                  Mobility, and the two incapacity in-kind fields.
