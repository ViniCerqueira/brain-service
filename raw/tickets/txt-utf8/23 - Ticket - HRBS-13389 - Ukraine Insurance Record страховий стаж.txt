Issue Description:

The field "Insurance Record" (страховий стаж) is missing from the Ukraine Data Dictionary (DD).

What this field is:
Insurance Record is the employee's accumulated length of social insurance service (страховий
стаж), as defined by Ukrainian labor legislation. It is a cumulative value carried over from the
employee's full work history, not something that can be calculated from data within a single
employer's system alone — it must be entered/updated at the time of hire based on the
employee's labor book / prior insurance record documentation.

Why it's required:
Insurance Record directly determines the sick pay percentage applied in temporary disability
calculations under Ukrainian law:

less than 5 years of insurance record → 60% of average earnings
5 to 8 years → 80% of average earnings
8+ years → 100% of average earnings

Without this field being correctly captured and stored, the system cannot apply the correct
percentage, resulting in incorrect sick leave payment calculations.

Current gap:
This field currently exists only in our Master Data upload template, under the column "Insurance
Record (for sick pay)". However, there is no corresponding field in the Data Dictionary. As a
result:

Impact if not addressed:

employment.
Manual workaround / data entry outside the system will be required, increasing risk of calculation
errors and compliance issues under Ukrainian labor law.
