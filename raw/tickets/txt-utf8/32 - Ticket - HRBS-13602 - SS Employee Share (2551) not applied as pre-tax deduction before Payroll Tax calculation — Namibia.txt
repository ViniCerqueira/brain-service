The SS Employee Share deduction (Wage Type 2551, capped at NAD 99/month) currently
reduces Net Pay correctly in the Gross-to-Net waterfall, but does not reduce the taxable base
used to calculate Payroll Tax (2552). Per Baker Hughes's own Wage Type Catalogue, the
documented Payroll Tax formula explicitly includes this as a pre-tax reduction ("Taxable base =
Basic + Housing ... − SS Ee Pension Add"). This has also been independently confirmed as
standard Namibian PAYE practice: statutory deductions including the SSC employee contribution
are meant to be subtracted from gross income before arriving at taxable income, ahead of the
PAYE calculation itself.

        Evidence: Isolated on a clean employee record (no bonus, no data-entry irregularities) — Suli
        Ndala, March 2026. Recurring pay = 46,212.91/month. If the 99 SS deduction were applied pre-
        tax as specified, monthly tax should be 9,667.51. HRB's actual output is 9,697.21 — a gap of
        exactly 29.70, which equals 99 × 30% (the marginal rate this employee's income falls into). The
        same 29.70 gap reproduces identically across every employee with clean, correctly-entered data
        (Tevin, Naem, Tomas), confirming this is systemic rather than employee-specific.

        Requested actions:

        Confirm with build team whether WT 2551 is flagged to reduce the taxable base for WT 2552's
        calculation, or only to reduce Net Pay.
