                 @Lily Li

                 For this requirement I need to understand the system behavior, like the non-taxable
                 amount — the system will exclude it from the taxable wage type, right? We do not need
                 to give a separate code, am I correct here? And for the taxable wage code, 4ДФ series
                 126 — so for the taxable benefit I can use the 126 4ДФ series.

                 The non-taxable part goes under code "160" and is treated as personified income of a
                 specific individual (tied to a named person's tax ID).

                 The taxable excess (the amount above the 25% threshold) is not reported under "160."
                 Instead it is taxed as an additional benefit (додаткове благо) and reported under a
                 different income code, typically "126."

                 So the "series" you're referring to isn't a range of codes — "160" is a single distinct income
                 code in the 4ДФ classification, and it pairs with code "126" when a gift exceeds the limit
                 and part becomes taxable.

                 Kind regards,
                 Vaishnavi Mane
