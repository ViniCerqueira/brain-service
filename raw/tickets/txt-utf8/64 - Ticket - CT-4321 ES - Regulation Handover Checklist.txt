        Wallisson dos Santos Gomes Commented 5 months ago
        @Lily Li The ES - Statutory Research sheet is for pay elements only — earnings,
        deductions, contribution bases, and employer contributions. That is what needs BA
        mapping (the wage type → payslip section mapping).

        The master data fields (company name, NIF, CCC, employee name, NAF, Grupo de
        Cotización, contract type, IRPF rate, etc.) are displayed in the España (EN) payslip layout
        sheet — the actual nómina. Those fields do not need mapping in the Statutory Research
        tab because they are not pay elements. They are already defined and mapped in the Data
        Dictionary (DD) — the fields are the same DD fields we've been working on throughout this
        project (e.g. $legal_entity_hr.tax_id_number_nif, $hr.social_security_affiliation_number,
        $hr.contribution_group, $legal_entity_hr.social_security_contribution_account_code, etc.).
        So to summarise what you need to do:

             ES - Statutory Research tab → map pay elements (wage types → payslip section,
             BCCC/BCCP/IRPF flags)
             España (EN) tab → the header fields are reference only; their source mappings come
             from the DD, no separate mapping needed in this sheet
