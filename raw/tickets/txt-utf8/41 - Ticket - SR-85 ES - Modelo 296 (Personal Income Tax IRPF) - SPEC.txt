        Each Type 2 record is either cash (D) or in-kind (E)... If an employee receives both cash
        and in-kind... you generate TWO separate Type 2 records." This implies the condition
        should run both ways:

             Cash income exists → generate D record
             In-kind income exists → generate E record

        Type 1 totals Type 2 records.

        An employee who receives only in-kind compensation with zero cash salary (e.g., a non-
        cash-only benefits arrangement) should get only an E record.

        An employee who receives only cash compensation with zero in-kind should get only a D
        record.
