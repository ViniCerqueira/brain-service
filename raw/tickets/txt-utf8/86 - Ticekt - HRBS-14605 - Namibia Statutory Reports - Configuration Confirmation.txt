For our Namibia entity, could you please confirm the current configuration status of the following
statutory reports:

1. Monthly PAYE Return - filed with the Namibia Revenue Agency (NamRA), due within 20 days
   after month end. Please confirm this is configured and the correct progressive tax bands are
   applied (note: Namibia's tax year runs March to February, not the calendar year - please
   confirm the system is aligned to this cycle).

2. SSC Contribution Return - monthly filing to the Social Security Commission. We have seen
   conflicting rate information in our research (some sources cite 0.9% employer/0.9% employee
   of insurable earnings capped around NAD 11,000/month salary, others cite a flat contribution
   amount). Could you confirm the exact rate/cap Mercans is currently applying for our
   employees?

3. VET Levy Return - the 1% Vocational Education and Training levy, employer-only, paid to the
   Namibia Training Authority, applicable if our total payroll is above the relevant threshold.
   Please confirm whether this applies to us and if so, that it is configured.

4. IT12E - annual employee tax certificates, and IT14E - the annual employer
   summary/reconciliation return to NamRA, both due by 30 June following the March-February
   tax year end. Please confirm these are configured and ready ahead of the relevant deadline.

If any of the above are not yet fully configured, could you confirm what is required on our end
and the expected turnaround time.
