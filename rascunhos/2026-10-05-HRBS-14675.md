Two answers. First, the 2026 percentages listed in the ticket are the correct ones, with one item we cannot match. **1. 2026 percentages (Belastingdienst, "Tarieven, bedragen en percentages loonheffingen vanaf 1 januari 2026", Cijferbijlage LH-209, table 9; Handboek Loonheffingen 2026)**

| Item in the ticket | Our 2026 value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| ZVW 6.10% | Werkgeversheffing Zvw 6.10% (employer levy) | Capped at the maximum contribution wage of EUR 79,409. It is paid by the employer and is not withheld from an ordinary active employee. The employee contribution of 4.85% is a different charge and cannot appear in the same wage calculation as the levy. |
| AOF Laag 6.27% | Aof laag 6.27% | Small employers: total premium wage of year t-2 (2024) up to EUR 1,082,500. |
| AOF Hoog 7.63% | Aof hoog 7.63% | Medium and large employers. |
| AWF Laag 2.74% | AWf laag 2.74% | Only if the contract is for an indefinite term, in writing, and not on-call (all three conditions). |
| AWF Hoog 7.74% | AWf hoog 7.74% | Applies otherwise. |
| WKO 0.50% | Opslag Wko 0.50% | Employer only. |
| ZW Flex, WGA "as per legal entity" | Gedifferentieerde premie Whk | Per the Belastingdienst decision for each entity; it can be negative. Correct to take it from the legal entity data. |
| **AOF Uit 7.63%** | **No counterpart** | We do not have an "Aof uit" premium in our 2026 research. Please tell us what this line represents. |

Aof laag or hoog and AWf laag or hoog depend on each entity, so the report cannot show a single rate for all six Omnicom entities without reading the category per legal entity.

**2. Is it a statutory report?** Our NL set contains: NL-RPT-001 Aangifte loonheffingen, 002 Correctiebericht, 003 Eindheffing WKR, 004 Pseudo-eindheffing excessieve vertrekvergoedingen, 005 Jaaropgaaf, 006 Loonstrook, 007 UPA and 008 Loonstaat. A PDF summary of social-security contributions by premium is not among them. It reads as a standard payroll report. The Reports team is right that it is configured as a custom report, and we have no specification to update it against.
