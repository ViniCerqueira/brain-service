**Reply (English):**

Hi team,

For Congo, there is no compliance objection to showing the three items on the payslip. Our research does not identify a provision that makes marital status and the number of children mandatory on the bulletin de paie, so they would be display fields. The number of parts is already part of our Congo payslip.

**What the Congo compliance payslip carries today**

- Our payslip specification has the line "Situation Familiale (Quotient)", mapped to the employee's number of family parts, with a cap of 6.5 parts (ITS family quotient, loi n°42-2025 Art. 116 A-F). In the specimen it is rendered as, for example, "Marié, 2 enfants - 3 parts", which already contains marital status, number of children and parts in one line.
- The mapping in the specification is the number of parts only. If the client wants marital status and number of children as separate fields, they would have to be added to the payslip layout, read from the employee's family situation (which is already a mandatory master-data field because it determines the parts).

**Why these data matter**

- The family situation is assessed as at 1 January of the tax year and sets the parts used for the ITS (loi n°42-2025 Art. 116 F). The employee must declare marital status and dependent children. Showing them on the payslip helps the employee check the ITS calculation.

**What is not confirmed**

- The bulletin de paie is mandatory under the Code du Travail (loi n°45-75 as amended by loi n°6-96), but our research does not list marital status or number of children among legally required items. If the client's tax advisor says the country recommendation requires them, please ask for the provision they cite, so we can read it in the source.

**Impact on artifacts**

- Payslip: change optional. If the client wants it, add two display fields (marital status, number of children) next to "Situation Familiale (Quotient)", sourced from the employee master data. This would be a layout change to the Congo payslip generator and Payslip Spec, to be planned and approved before editing.
- CCG, WTC, DD: no change (Family Situation is already a mandatory DD field).

**What we need from the client**

- Whether they want the three items as separate fields or the single line already in our specimen.
- The provision cited by their tax advisor, if it is presented as mandatory.

Kind regards,
Wallisson
