**Reply (English):**

Hi team,

Items 1 to 4 of your description are correct, with one wording fix in item 4. Item 5 (IBC during vacation) we cannot confirm: it contradicts what our research found, so we need the source you rely on.

**1. IBC (confirmed).** Floor 1 SMMLV = COP 1,750,905 (Decree 0159 of 19-02-2026, which re-fixed the amount transitorily after the Consejo de Estado provisionally suspended Decree 1469 of 2025; the value can change when the court rules on the merits). Ceiling 25 SMMLV = COP 43,772,625; integral salary contributes on 70% (Ley 100 of 1993 art. 18, as replaced by Ley 797 of 2003 art. 5).

**2. 40% rule (confirmed, with scope).** Ley 1393 of 2010 art. 30 caps non-salary payments at 40% of total remuneration for social security purposes (arts. 18 and 204 of Ley 100); the excess goes back into the IBC. It applies only to payments agreed as non-salary under CST art. 128 that would otherwise remunerate the service. Payments that are non-salary by nature (occasional travel expenses, work tools, dotación, liberalities, indemnities) are left out of both sides of the test (Consejo de Estado, Sección Cuarta, unification ruling rad. 05001-23-33-000-2016-02496-01 (25185), 09-12-2021). The cap does not change the salary base for prestaciones sociales.

**3. Rates (confirmed).**
- Health 12.5%: employee 4%, employer 8.5% (Ley 100 art. 204, as amended by Ley 1122 of 2007 art. 10).
- Pension 16%: employee 4%, employer 12% (Ley 100 art. 20, as replaced by Ley 797 of 2003 art. 7; Decree 4982 of 2007).
- ARL, employer only: 0.348% to 8.7% of the IBC by risk class; initial values 0.522% (I), 1.044% (II), 2.436% (III), 4.350% (IV), 6.960% (V); class IV maximum is 6.060% (Decree 1772 of 1994 art. 13; Decree 1072 of 2015 art. 2.2.4.3.4).
- Fondo de Solidaridad Pensional, employee only and additional to the 4%: 1% from 4 SMMLV; plus the subsistence sub-account of 0.2%, 0.4%, 0.6%, 0.8% for 16-17, 17-18, 18-19, 19-20 SMMLV and 1% above 20 SMMLV (Ley 100 art. 20). Total 1.0% to 2.0%.

**4. Parafiscales and exoneration (confirmed, with one correction).** CCF 4%, SENA 2%, ICBF 3%, employer only. Base is the monthly payroll of salaries including paid rest days (Ley 21 of 1982 art. 17), with **no 25 SMMLV ceiling** and not the IBC. Correction: the exoneration of employer health 8.5%, SENA and ICBF (ET art. 114-1) applies to employees earning **less than** 10 SMMLV (below COP 17,509,050), not "up to" 10. At exactly 10 SMMLV it is lost. The CCF 4% and the employer pension 12% are never exonerated. The test is per employee, per month, and requires an employer that is a legal entity declaring income tax (or a natural person with two or more employees).

**5. IBC during vacation (not confirmed).** Our research states that vacation pay is part of the IBC and of the parafiscal base, and that contributions continue during leave (Ley 21 of 1982 art. 17; CST art. 192 for the vacation pay itself). We did not find the rule you describe (IBC = salary of the month before the vacation, for both enjoyed and advance-paid vacations). The citation "Art. 3, Decree 1072 of 2015" does not match an article we can locate in that decree, whose numbering is 2.2.x. Please send the exact article or the PILA instruction you rely on, and the validator case with 4 days of leave. We will not state either position until we have read the primary source.
