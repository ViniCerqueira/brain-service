**Reply (English):**

Hi team,

The statutory rules described in the ticket are correct, with two additions that affect how the consolidation report must be built (the fund deposit and the base). The provision accounting (22110, 22112) and the consolidation report are not statutory reports; they need a specification from the client's current Excel.

**Confirmed**

- **Cesantías:** one month of salary per year of service, proportional for a fraction of a year (CST art. 249).
- **Base:** the last monthly salary if it has not varied in the last three months; otherwise, and for variable salary, the average of the last year of service, or of the whole period if shorter (CST art. 253). The ticket's reference to Article 253 is right.
- **Interest on cesantías:** 12% per year on the balance at 31 December, or at the date of termination or partial liquidation, proportional to the elapsed time (Ley 52 of 1975 art. 1); paid directly to the employee in January of the following year, or at termination (Ley 52 art. 1 num. 2). If unpaid, the employer owes an indemnity equal to the interest (art. 1 num. 3). The ticket's formula (accrued severance x 12% x days / 360) is consistent with our accrual convention of 1.0% of the base per month (12% x 8.3333%).
- **Monthly accrual:** cesantías 8.3333% and interest 1.0% of the prestaciones base.

**Two additions for the consolidation report**

1. **The cesantías principal is not paid to the employee; it is deposited.** Under the annual regime (Ley 50 of 1990 art. 99) the severance is liquidated at 31 December and deposited in the employee's chosen cesantías fund before 15 February (by 14 February); late deposit costs one day of salary per day of delay. Direct payment to the employee releases the obligation only in the case created by Ley 2466 of 2025 art. 15 (CST art. 57 par. 4), if it is duly proven, and fund affiliation remains mandatory. The report columns "payments or settlements" should therefore separate fund deposits, direct payments and interest payments. Interest is the only item paid directly in January.
2. **Base:** our CCG includes the transport allowance in the prestaciones base for cesantías and interest, as settled practice and not statute. Please confirm that the client's calculation does the same.

**Tax:** cesantías and their interest are left out of the monthly withholding base. They are exempt from income tax when the average monthly income of the last six months of employment does not exceed 350 UVT (COP 18,330,900 in 2026), with the tapering table above that (ET art. 206 num. 4).

**Impact on artifacts**

- CCG: no change.
- WTC: 22110 and 22112 are the client's provision concepts. We have not mapped them to a statutory wage type; the system needs an automatic provision per the accrual rates above.
- Report: the severance consolidation report is not one of our Colombia statutory reports (CO-RPT-001 to 007); the cesantías fund deposit allocation is CO-RPT-006 and is not specified. A new report specification is needed, built from the Excel attached to the ticket. No spec exists yet.

**What we need from the client**

- Confirm the base includes the transport allowance.
- Confirm that the entity runs only the annual regime (employees hired from 1 January 1991) or list any employees under the old regime.
- Confirm that the "interest payments" column in the consolidation is the January payment.
