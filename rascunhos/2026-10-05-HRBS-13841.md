**Reply (English):**

Hi Daniel,

Yes. The CAMU contributions of 4.55% (employer) and 2.27% (employee) are statutory, both on gross salary, and they are separate from the 0.5% solidarity contribution confirmed in my previous comment.

**Legal basis and rates**

- Employer 4.55% and employee 2.27% of gross salary, 6.82% combined, collected through the CNSS circuit: Décret n°2024-133 of 27-03-2024 (CAMU). Registration for CAMU runs through the CNSS (Décret n°2024-132 of 27-03-2024). The rates also appear on the CAMU authority's site (camu-congo.fr), as quoted in my earlier reply.
- Solidarity contribution: 0.5% on the fraction of gross salary above 500,000 XAF. It is a different funding stream, not part of the 2.27%. This is the item that appears in the client's WTC.
- So the client's WTC line (about 0.5% of earnings) corresponds only to the solidarity contribution. The 4.55% and 2.27% are not in it.

**Caveat on the sources.** Our research flags the CAMU rates of Décret n°2024-133 as pending a literal read of the scanned decree. The rates above come from our CCG and the CAMU authority's site; I recommend that Baker Hughes confirm them with the CAMU or the CNSS if they need a document for audit.

**Impact on artifacts**

- Compliance WTC (CG-WTC-001 v1.0, as in our research set): it carries CG-13 (CAMU employee 2.27%), CG-17 (CAMU employer 4.55%) and CG-18 (CAMU solidarity 0.5% above 500,000). If the WTC you are looking at does not show them, please send the link or version, because it may not be the research version.
- Gross-to-net: the HRB G2N applies 2.27% to the employee, which is correct. The client's G2N, which has no 2.27%, would under-deduct the employee and under-report the employer's 4.55%.

**What we need from the client**

- Whether the 2.27% and 4.55% are being processed outside payroll (for example directly through the CNSS bordereau), or not at all. If they are not being processed, the client's statutory position needs review by their advisor.

Kind regards,
