Hi team,

The validator follows the statutory sequence for employment-income withholding, and most of the parameters are correct for 2026. Five points need to be corrected or confirmed before we use it as the reference for the gap analysis (points 1 to 5 below).

**Confirmed against our Colombia research**

- UVT 2026 = COP 52,374 (DIAN Resolution 000238 of 15-12-2025).
- Table in UVT (ET art. 383, as replaced by Ley 2010 of 2019 art. 42): 0-95 UVT 0%; >95-150 19%; >150-360 28% (+10 UVT); >360-640 33% (+69); >640-945 35% (+162); >945-2,300 37% (+268); above 2,300 39% (+770). The band boundaries in COP shown in the ticket are right, except the 945 UVT row (point 1).
- Base depuration (ET art. 388, art. 387, art. 206 num. 10): mandatory health and pension (including FSP) are deducted first as income not constituting income; then the art. 387 deductions (prepaid health 16 UVT/month; dependents 10% of the month's labour income, maximum 32 UVT/month; housing-loan interest, monthly cap 100 UVT, which our CCG flags as derived and not verbatim); then voluntary pension/AFC (ET art. 126-1, 30% of labour income); then the 25% exempt income (ET art. 206 num. 10, annual cap 790 UVT), which must be calculated last, after all other deductions and exempt income.
- Combined limit: deductions plus exempt income cannot exceed 40% of the base after income not constituting income, nor 1,340 UVT per year (ET art. 388; Decree 1625 of 2016 art. 1.2.4.1.6 par. 3, as amended by Decree 2231 of 2023 art. 9).
- Meal payments to third parties (ET art. 387-1): not income for the employee if salary does not exceed 310 UVT; the excess over 41 UVT per month is taxable labour income.
- Procedure 1 (ET art. 385) and Procedure 2 (ET art. 386, fixed percentage recalculated in June and December and applied for the following six months).

**To correct or confirm**

1. **Row 945 UVT:** 945 x 52,374 = COP 49,493,430. The ticket shows 49,483,530 (digits transposed). Please check the cell in the validator.
2. **Procedure 2 divisor:** ET art. 386 divides the total taxable payments of the previous 12 months by **13** (or by the actual number of months of employment if under 12 months), excluding cesantías and their interest. The ticket describes an average over 12 months. Please confirm which divisor "Recaculo Retencio" uses; if it is 12 the fixed percentage will be overstated.
3. **Service bonus (prima) under Procedure 1:** ET art. 385 establishes that the legal minimum prima is withheld separately, reading the table against the prima itself, and not added to the month's salary. Our research does not confirm the ticket's statement that mandatory contributions, art. 387 deductions and the 40% limit are not applied to the prima, nor that only the 25% exempt income is deducted. Please send the DIAN concept the client relies on so we can read it in the source.
4. **1,340 UVT limit applied as 111.67 UVT per month:** the statutory limit is annual. The client's own observation is right: the monthly approximation can under-withhold when the annual limit has already been reached. A year-to-date control is needed in the system design.
5. **Additional dependent deduction:** ET art. 336 num. 3 allows an additional 72 UVT per dependent, maximum four dependents, **outside** the 40% and 1,340 UVT limit, and Decree 2231 of 2023 art. 3 allows it together with the art. 387 dependent deduction for employees. It is not among the thresholds listed in the ticket. Please confirm whether the validator applies it.

**Outside what we can confirm**

- The 20% flat withholding for employees classified as Expat Home (WT 21330) and the "Contingente" sheet are not covered by our Colombia research. We cannot confirm their legal basis. The client remains responsible for the tax status of each employee, and any change of method needs the client's written instruction, as the ticket already states.
